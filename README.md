# vergote

Website for **Tuinen Vergote Mathieu** (garden design and maintenance, Gits): https://www.tuinenvergotemathieu.be. **Live since 20 November 2026**, published before Mathieu reviewed the text (the owner's decision); see the lists below.

Plain HTML + CSS, no build step, served by GitHub Pages. Text is in Dutch.

Shared style: `assets/css/style.css`. Menu script: `assets/js/site.js`. Images: `assets/img/`.

**Editing:** all pages are generated by `tools/build.py` (recipes in `tools/recepten_data.py`). Change the script, run `python3 tools/build.py .`, then check with `python3 tools/check_site.py` (with `python3 -m http.server 8793` running). Don't edit the HTML by hand: the next build overwrites it.

## Design

The feel is an early morning in a natural garden: lots of space, a slow rhythm, soft light. The look was inspired by the photo-led layout of tuinenvanlerberghe.be, made calmer: no pop-ups, no cookie banner, more space.

- **Colours:** dew `#F7F8F4` (page), sage `#E4EADF`, moss `#3E5641` / `#2B3B2D`, saffron gold `#9A6F22` (underlines, numerals), crocus `#6E5A9B` (saffron only).
- **Type:** Fraunces (soft, light) for headings and Figtree for text. Both are self-hosted in `assets/fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License; the `OFL-*.txt` files must stay next to them.
- **Motion:** only the home photo settles slowly on load, and not at all for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- **Menu:** a native popover that collapses only when the links don't fit; older browsers fall back to a simple toggle.

## "Nu in de tuin" (automatic, follows the month)

- **Own page `tuinjaar.html`** (moved off the home page on 2026-11-20: "that breaks the page calmness"). Linked quietly from the home intro ("Wat er nu in de tuin gebeurt") and the footer of every page, not from the menu. It shows the current month and season. It has a short note on what happens in the garden now, with a link to the matching service. The section's background shifts with the season (winter cool, spring green, summer warm, autumn ochre). A month strip below lets visitors peek at other months; a dot marks today.
- **Saffraan page:** one line on where the saffron is in its year ("Nu, in oktober: Oogsttijd…").
- **Content:** `tools/tuinjaar_data.py` (12 notes plus 12 saffron lines). Checked against Flemish/Dutch garden calendars and ANB advice on hedges (not during the breeding season, roughly mid-March to mid-August). Keep each note to a title and at most two sentences.
- All 12 months are in the HTML (for search engines, and as a fallback without JS); `site.js` shows the current one. Preview with `?maand=1` … `?maand=12`.

## Seasonal touch (automatic, every year)

Set by `assets/js/site.js` on the visitor's date; no deploy needed.
- **7–26 December:** three red holly berries on the logo sprig, a small translucent pill "Fijne feestdagen" (holly icon) above the home headline, and "Mathieu wenst u fijne feestdagen." in the footer.
- **27 December – 15 January:** a gold star on the logo, a pill "Gelukkig nieuwjaar" (star icon) above the home headline, and "Gelukkig nieuwjaar! Mathieu wenst u een groen en gezond <year>." in the footer (the year fills itself in).
- The user asked for "a very small touch, not a full-blown Christmas site", then "a little more, not too much": keep it at this level.
- Preview at any time with `?seizoen=kerst`, `?seizoen=nieuwjaar` or `?seizoen=geen`.

## Main address: www

`www.tuinenvergotemathieu.be` is the main address (the user's choice). The bare domain, http and every page path redirect there with a 301. `tools/build.py` holds it in one place, `SITE`, and from it writes the canonical and og:url tags, the JSON-LD, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and `CNAME`. `robots.txt` blocks `/tools/` and `/README.md`, which are served publicly but are not pages. Changing the custom domain via the API turns off `https_enforced`: switch it back on afterwards.
